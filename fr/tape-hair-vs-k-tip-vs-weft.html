Tape-in vs K-tip vs Weft

Choisissez la bonne méthode d’extension pour votre marché.

Résumé acheteur : Comparez tape-in, K-tip et weft du point de vue de l’acheteur professionnel : application, entretien, MOQ et marge.

Start with the professional using the product

A method should not be selected only because it is popular. Map the training level, application time, maintenance cycle and removal workflow of the salons or stylists you serve. A product that does not fit their working method creates support problems even when the hair itself is good.

Tape hair: efficient application and a system-led reorder

Tape systems can suit buyers who want a relatively fast salon application and a clear consumables cycle. The commercial brief should cover panel construction, weight, tape compatibility, shade system and replacement tabs—not just length and colour.

K-tips: detailed application and strand-level flexibility

K-tip ranges are often evaluated around bond construction, strand consistency, application tools and stylist technique. Buyers should compare how the bond behaves during installation and wear, then document the approved reference for future orders.

Wefts: construction choice changes the offer

Machine, hand-tied and thinner weft constructions can serve different installation approaches and price positions. Compare seam profile, shedding control, flexibility, density and how the weft will be cut or installed.

Build the sample around a decision

Avoid requesting random samples. Choose representative shades and specifications that answer a real range decision. Record what your team approved, what needs correction and which details must remain consistent on reorder.

Questions fréquentes

Quelle méthode d’extension est la meilleure pour une nouvelle gamme en gros ?

Il n’existe pas de méthode universellement meilleure. Le bon point de départ dépend de votre base de stylistes, modèle client, capacité de service et positionnement prix.

Chaque méthode devrait-elle utiliser la même gamme de couleurs ?

Une architecture de tons de base partagée peut simplifier l’offre, mais la disponibilité et les résultats visuels doivent être examinés sur chaque construction.
