CONNAISSANCE PROFESSIONNELLE DU PRODUIT

Utilisez la page pour prendre une meilleure décision d’achat.

Conseils pratiques pour les salons, distributeurs et équipes private label. Les affirmations spécifiques au produit nécessitent l’approbation de l’échantillon.

01

What is a nano ring extension?

A nano ring extension locks a Remy hair strand to the client’s own hair with a 0.9mm aluminium ring crimped by a plier. There is no glue and no heat, which is why it is pitched as the most discreet, damage-lowest bond.

02

Does nano ring damage hair?

Correctly fitted, a nano ring applies no glue or heat and can be removed without cutting the strand, so the damage risk is among the lowest of all methods. Damage comes from over-tight rings, wrong size or poor aftercare, not the method itself.

03

How long do nano rings last?

A nano install is typically moved up every 6–8 weeks as the client’s hair grows. The strand itself is reused across several move-ups until the hair quality calls for replacement.

04

Nano ring vs micro ring?

A nano ring is about 0.9mm against a micro ring’s 1.2mm — smaller, flatter and less visible, which is why nano is preferred for fine and thinning hair. Micro ring suits normal hair and is slightly faster to fit.

05

How many grams make a full head?

A full nano head often uses 100–150g total depending on hair thickness and desired volume. About 150–200 strands at 0.5–1.0g each is typical; finer hair needs fewer, denser hair needs more. Final count depends on the client’s own hair.

06

What controls a reliable reorder?

Keep one approved strand and one written specification covering ring size, tip type, grams per strand, length, texture, colour reference, tolerances, packaging and QC checkpoints.

QUESTIONS DE L’ACHETEUR

Avant de demander un devis

Do Nano Hair Extensions have a product code?

No. This method family is identified by its ring size and confirmed specification: nano 0.9mm (or fine-hair 0.7mm) ring, loose or pre-tipped I-tip, followed by grams per strand, length, hair grade, colour and packaging.

What is the difference between nano ring, micro ring and keratin?

Nano ring (0.9mm) and micro ring (1.2mm) both crimp a metal ring with no glue or heat and reuse the strand; nano is smaller and best for fine hair. Keratin (K-tip) melts glue with heat, consumes the tip and cannot reuse the strand.

Can nano ring be used on thin or fine hair?

Yes — that is its main advantage. The 0.9mm ring sits flatter and is less visible than a micro ring, and a 0.7mm fine-hair ring serves very fragile strands. No glue or heat means lower damage risk than bonded methods.

How many grams make a full head?

Approximately 100–150g total depending on hair thickness and desired volume. About 150–200 strands at 0.5–1.0g each is typical. Final count depends on the client’s own hair density and the result wanted.

Can I request a nano ring sample?

Yes. Share the preferred ring size, tip type, grams per strand, length, hair grade, colour direction and strand count. Sample feasibility, cost and timing are confirmed against that brief.

Are all lengths and weights shown ready to order?

No. They are buying-request options for preparing a brief. A grams and length value is not confirmed until it is tied to the chosen ring size, hair specification and written quotation.

Can DS HAIR develop rooted, ombre or textured nano ring?

Yes. State the root shadow depth and end colour, or the texture (Yaki, deep wave, Afro curl) and colour blend, so the construction can be evaluated and sampled.
