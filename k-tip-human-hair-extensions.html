PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

Use the page to make a better buying decision.

Practical guidance for salons, distributors and private-label teams. Product-specific claims still require sample approval.

01

What types of K-tip extensions are available?

The range can be developed as K-tip (round fusion), V-tip (flat fusion), U-tip (flat fusion) or I-tip (cold micro-link). Each format serves a different application method, tool and wearer profile.

02

How many K-tip strands does a client need?

Fine hair may need 80–100 strands for a full look, medium hair 120–160 strands and thick hair 180–220 strands. Two strands are applied per small section; the total depends on desired density and length.

03

How do K-tip and I-tip differ?

K-tip uses a heat-fused keratin bond applied with a fusion connector. I-tip is a cold method attached with a silicone-lined micro-link ring and pliers. I-tip is reusable; K-tip is re-tipped after removal.

04

Why choose flat V-tip for fine hair?

A flattened, lower-profile keratin bond sits closer to the scalp and shows less through thin hair. Translucent or scalp-matched bond colour further reduces visibility at the root.

05

How long do K-tip extensions last?

With professional application and care, K-tip fusion extensions typically wear 3–6 months before move-up or re-tip. Lifespan depends on hair grade, aftercare and the wearer's natural growth.

06

What controls a reliable reorder?

Keep one approved sample and one written specification covering tip format, strand weight, bond colour, hair details, length, texture, colour reference, tolerances, packaging and QC checkpoints.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote

Do K-Tip Hair Extensions have a product code?

No. This method family is identified by its bond format and confirmed specification: K-tip, I-tip, U-tip or V-tip, followed by strand weight, length, hair grade, colour and packaging.

What is the difference between K-tip, I-tip, U-tip and V-tip?

K-tip and U-tip are heat-fused keratin bonds (round and flat); V-tip is a flat fusion bond for fine hair; I-tip is a cold micro-link applied with a ring and pliers. Each uses a different tool and suits a different wearer profile.

Can I request a K-tip sample?

Yes. Share the preferred tip format, strand weight, length, hair grade, colour direction and pack count. Sample feasibility, cost and timing are confirmed against that brief.

How many strands make a full head?

Approximately 80–100 strands for fine hair, 120–160 for medium hair and 180–220 for thick hair. Final count depends on density, length and desired result.

Can DS HAIR develop flat V-tip or textured K-tip?

Yes. State the target bond flatness and translucency, or the texture (Yaki, deep wave, Afro curl), so the construction can be evaluated.

Are all lengths and weights shown ready to order?

No. They are buying-request options for preparing a brief. A strand weight and length value is not confirmed until it is tied to the chosen tip format, hair specification and written quotation.

Can I request private-label packaging?

Yes. Label, backing card, storage box, re-tip kit and retail-pack requirements can be reviewed with the product brief. Feasibility and minimum quantities are confirmed separately.
