# WigExporter SEO/GEO baseline

Updated: 2026-07-26

## Business and conversion boundary

- Audience: hair brands, distributors, salons and professional buyers.
- Primary conversion: qualified RFQ / sourcing brief.
- Secondary conversions: sample request, trade-account application, free colour-kit request and WhatsApp conversation.
- No cart, checkout, retail pricing or consumer promotion.

## Organic architecture

- Home: global B2B hair sourcing proposition.
- Collections hub: product-family navigation.
- Five commercial collection pages: one clear purchase-intent cluster per page.
- Six human-hair extension method pages (clip-in, tape-in, K-tip, genius weft, machine weft, nano ring) — process pages, no SKU.
- Sixteen product pages: 4 lace wigs (DS-WIG-LW-001–004) and 12 human hair toppers (DS-TOP-HH-001–012).
- Ten synthetic hairpiece product pages (DS-HPC-SY / DS-TOP-SY-CI).
- Trade services: sampling, customisation, trade account, colour kits, colour & packaging studio.
- Buyer guides: answer real sourcing and product-evaluation questions, then hand off to the relevant commercial page.

## Technical SEO

- `sitemap.xml` is generated by `scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` during `npm run build` (scans every root `*.html`; emits `lastmod`, `changefreq`, `priority`). It can never go stale.
- `robots.txt` allows all and points to the sitemap.
- Every page exposes: unique `<title>` (≤60 chars), `meta description` (≤160 chars), `canonical`, `robots=index,follow`, Open Graph (`og:title/description/type/url/site_name/locale/image/image:alt`) and Twitter card (`twitter:card/title/description/image`).
- Asset references carry a unified cache-busting version (`?v=20260726-9`); bump it whenever CSS/JS change so returning visitors receive the latest build.
- `vercel.json` sends `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy`, `Strict-Transport-Security` (HSTS, preload) and `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
- Images use `loading="lazy"` (except the LCP image) and `decoding="async"` to protect Core Web Vitals.

## Structured data (JSON-LD)

- Home: `Organization` (`#organization`, with `logo`, `image`, `sameAs` → dshairbeauty.co.uk) + `WebSite` (`#website`).
- Product pages: `Product` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- Collection pages: `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- Article pages: `Article` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- Static hub/utility pages: `CollectionPage` / `AboutPage` / `ContactPage` / `WebPage`, each `isPartOf` `#website` and `publisher` `#organization`.
- No fabricated `offers`/`aggregateRating` — pricing is enquiry-led.

## GEO requirements

- `llms.txt` is the primary machine-readable map for AI crawlers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini). It lists every collection, method page, product page, synthetic page, service and guide, with one-line context and the B2B boundary stated up front.
- Important answers are visible in HTML, not hidden in images or client-only widgets (FAQ sections, buyer-answer blocks, evaluation points).
- Entity clarity: `DS HAIR` = brand, `WigExporter` = global B2B site, `D.S Hair Beauty` / dshairbeauty.co.uk = UK salon brand. These are declared in Organization `alternateName` and `sameAs`.
- Claims about history, manufacturing ownership, certification, capacity, MOQ and lead time must not be published without evidence.

## Measurement required after launch

- Form-submit success and failure events.
- Clicks on email, telephone and WhatsApp.
- Trade Account, Free Color Kit and Sample form submissions as separate conversions.
- Search Console (submit the regenerated sitemap) and analytics verification.
- Monthly review of queries, indexed pages, qualified leads and assisted conversions.
