PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

Use the page to make a better buying decision.

Practical guidance for salons, distributors and private-label teams. Product-specific claims still require sample approval.

01

What types of machine weft are available?

The range can be developed as standard machine weft, skinny machine weft, double or triple weft, and in textured or extra-long formats. Each serves a different price point, seam profile and wearer result.

02

Can machine weft be cut to size?

Yes. A machine-sewn track can be cut to the exact width needed and resealed at the cut end, then sewn in, taped or micro-linked. This is a key advantage over hand-tied weft, which sheds if cut.

03

How many grams make a full head?

A full head often uses 100–200 g total depending on desired volume and length. Approx. 2–3 packs (50–100 g each) add volume; 4–6 packs build a full-head look. Final count depends on width, density and result.

04

Machine weft vs hand-tied weft?

Machine weft has a thicker, machine-sewn track that is durable, cuttable and lower cost. Hand-tied weft is ultra-thin and seamless but cannot be cut and costs more. Genius weft offers a thin, cuttable hybrid.

05

How long does machine weft last?

With professional install and care, a machine weft can be reused across multiple installs (move-up or reinstall) thanks to its reinforced track. Lifespan depends on hair grade, aftercare and the wearer's natural growth.

06

What controls a reliable reorder?

Keep one approved sample and one written specification covering track type, grams per pack, width, hair details, length, texture, colour reference, tolerances, packaging and QC checkpoints.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote

Do Machine Weft Hair Extensions have a product code?

No. This method family is identified by its track construction and confirmed specification: standard, skinny, double or triple machine weft, followed by grams per pack, width, length, hair grade, colour and packaging.

What is the difference between machine weft, hand-tied weft and genius weft?

Machine weft is a machine-sewn reinforced track that is durable, cuttable and cost-effective. Hand-tied weft is an ultra-thin hand-sewn row that cannot be cut. Genius weft is a single-line machine weft that is thin like hand-tied yet can be cut.

Can machine weft be cut to the size I need?

Yes. The machine-sewn track can be cut to width and resealed, then sewn in, taped or micro-linked. Hand-tied weft should not be cut because it sheds at the cut edge.

How many grams make a full head?

Approximately 100–200 g total depending on volume and length. About 2–3 packs (50–100 g each) add volume; 4–6 packs build a full head. Final count depends on width, density and desired result.

Can I request a machine weft sample?

Yes. Share the preferred track type, grams per pack, width, length, hair grade, colour direction and pack count. Sample feasibility, cost and timing are confirmed against that brief.

Can DS HAIR develop skinny, ombre or textured machine weft?

Yes. State the target track thinness and stitch density, or the texture (Yaki, deep wave, Afro curl) and colour blend, so the construction can be evaluated.

Are all lengths and weights shown ready to order?

No. They are buying-request options for preparing a brief. A grams and length value is not confirmed until it is tied to the chosen track type, hair specification and written quotation.
