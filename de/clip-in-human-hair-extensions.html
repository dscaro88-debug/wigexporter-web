FACHWISSEN ZUM PRODUKT

Nutzen Sie die Seite für eine bessere Kaufentscheidung.

Praktische Hinweise für Salons, Distributoren und Private-Label-Teams. Produktspezifische Behauptungen erfordern die Mustergenehmigung.

01

What types of human hair clip-ins are available?

The range can be developed as classic sewn-weft sets, seamless PU sets, one-piece volumizers or individual fill-in pieces. Each type serves a different wearer profile and coverage goal.

02

How do you match gram weight to hair density?

For fine or thin hair, suggest 100–140 g sets to avoid traction and visible bulk. For medium-density hair, 160–180 g usually blends well. For thick or voluminous hair, 200 g or more is needed for a natural end result.

03

Where should colour be matched?

Match clip-ins to the root shade for the attachment points, then layer in a second set for highlights or balayage if needed. For highlighted hair, combining a root-tone set with a lighter-tone set creates the most natural blend.

04

Why is piece count not enough?

Two seven-piece sets can behave very differently when their widths, clip counts, total grams and grams-by-piece are different. A repeatable product requires a written set map, not only 7 pcs/set on the package.

05

How do classic and seamless clip-ins differ?

Classic clip-ins use a sewn or fabric weft with a more structured top. Seamless clip-ins use a thinner PU-style top designed for a flatter root profile. The correct choice depends on wearer density, target result and sample approval.

06

What controls a reliable reorder?

Keep one approved sample and one written specification covering the set map, grams by piece, PU construction, hair details, colour reference, clip hardware, tolerances, packaging and QC checkpoints.

KÄUFERFRAGEN

Bevor Sie ein Angebot anfordern

Do Clip-In Hair Extensions have a product code?

No. This product family is identified by its product name and confirmed specification: classic, seamless, one-piece or single clip-in, followed by the set map, length, weight distribution, colour and packaging.

Can I request a 7-piece or 8-piece sample?

Yes. Share the preferred format, length, target total weight, construction and colour direction. Sample feasibility, cost and timing are confirmed against that brief.

Does 7 pcs/set or 8 pcs/set define the full product?

No. The full specification also needs the construction, each piece width, clip layout, total hair weight, grams by piece, length, texture, colour reference and repeat-order tolerances.

Can DS HAIR develop a single clip-in piece?

A single-piece request can be reviewed. State the intended placement, width, clip count, target weight, length and colour so the construction can be evaluated.

Are all lengths and weights shown ready to order?

No. They are buying-request options for preparing a brief. A length and gram value is not confirmed until it is tied to the chosen product type, set map, hair specification and written quotation.

Can I request private-label packaging?

Yes. Label, backing card, storage and retail-pack requirements can be reviewed with the product brief. Feasibility and minimum quantities are confirmed separately.
