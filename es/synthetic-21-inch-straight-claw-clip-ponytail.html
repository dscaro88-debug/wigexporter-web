CONOCIMIENTO PROFESIONAL DEL PRODUCTO

Use la página para tomar una mejor decisión de compra.

Orientación práctica para salones, distribuidores y equipos de marca privada. Las afirmaciones específicas del producto requieren la aprobación de la muestra.

01

What is a straight claw-clip ponytail hairpiece?

It is a removable ponytail built around a clip that grips a secured natural ponytail or bun. The source reference combines a 21-inch straight fall with a claw-clip attachment.

02

What should buyers verify on the clip?

Check curve, tooth geometry, spring force, coating, colour, attachment stitching and how the base conceals the wearer’s natural hair.

03

How should a straight finish be approved?

Record strand alignment, layer placement, end density, fibre shine, movement and recovery after packing, brushing and the approved care routine.

04

Can heat-friendly synthetic hair be restyled?

Only within the tested protocol for the actual production fibre. The source describes flat-ironing, curling and blow-drying, but DS HAIR must validate its own sample before publishing a temperature limit.

05

What supports reliable reorders?

Retain an approved sample and written specification covering fibre, length, grams, straight finish, clip, base, colour, care wording, packaging and QC tolerances.

PREGUNTAS DEL COMPRADOR

Antes de solicitar una cotización

Is this 21-inch straight ponytail available for wholesale and private label?

It is presented as a B2B development reference. Share the target market, quantity, colour direction, fibre requirement and packaging brief so feasibility and commercial terms can be confirmed.

How does the reference ponytail attach?

The source product uses a secure claw clip. DS HAIR production clip dimensions, spring force, tooth finish and holding performance require sample approval.

What length and texture are documented?

The source page states a 21-inch length with a sleek straight finish. Finished measurement tolerance and end-density specification are confirmed on the production sample.

Can this synthetic ponytail be heat styled?

The source describes heat-friendly synthetic hair that may be flat-ironed, curled or blown out. That is not automatically a DS HAIR production claim; the selected fibre and sample must pass a documented heat test first.

Can buyers request custom colours and packaging?

Colour matching and private-label presentation can be reviewed against physical references, artwork and quantity requirements. Available options and minimums require written confirmation.
