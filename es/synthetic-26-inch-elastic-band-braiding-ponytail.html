CONOCIMIENTO PROFESIONAL DEL PRODUCTO

Use la página para tomar una mejor decisión de compra.

Orientación práctica para salones, distribuidores y equipos de marca privada. Las afirmaciones específicas del producto requieren la aprobación de la muestra.

01

What is an elastic-band braiding ponytail?

It is a removable length piece attached around a secured natural ponytail with an elastic band, then braided together with the wearer's hair.

02

Why is there a shorter hair section?

The source reference uses a shorter section to wrap around and conceal the elastic attachment after the main length is secured.

03

What should buyers test on the band?

Check dimensions, stretch recovery, seam security, comfort, grip and whether the wrapped finish stays concealed during normal movement.

04

Can heat-friendly synthetic hair be restyled?

Only within the tested protocol for the actual production fibre. Source wording does not establish a DS HAIR temperature limit.

05

What supports reliable reorders?

Retain an approved sample and written specification covering fibre, length, grams, band, wrap section, colour, care wording, packaging and QC tolerances.

PREGUNTAS DEL COMPRADOR

Antes de solicitar una cotización

Is this 26-inch braiding ponytail available for wholesale and private label?

It is presented as a B2B development reference. Share the target market, quantity, colour direction, fibre requirement and packaging brief so feasibility and commercial terms can be confirmed.

How does the reference ponytail attach?

The source product wraps an elastic band around a secured natural ponytail, then uses a shorter hair section to conceal the connection point.

What length and styling use are documented?

The source page states a 26-inch length designed to braid with natural hair or twist into a braided updo. Production measurement and tolerance require sample approval.

Can this synthetic ponytail be heat styled?

The source describes heat-friendly synthetic hair. That is not automatically a DS HAIR production claim; the selected fibre and sample must pass a documented heat test first.

Can buyers request custom colours and packaging?

Colour matching and private-label presentation can be reviewed against physical references, artwork and quantity requirements. Available options and minimums require written confirmation.
