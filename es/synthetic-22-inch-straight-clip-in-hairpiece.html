CONOCIMIENTO PROFESIONAL DEL PRODUCTO

Use la página para tomar una mejor decisión de compra.

Orientación práctica para salones, distribuidores y equipos de marca privada. Las afirmaciones específicas del producto requieren la aprobación de la muestra.

01

What is a one-piece clip-in hairpiece?

It is a wide hair section designed to add length and back-of-head coverage in one placement. This source reference uses seven pressure-sensitive clips across an 11 × 6.5-inch base.

02

What should buyers verify on the base?

Check curvature, edge finish, stitching, visibility, flexibility and how the base distributes weight. Dimensions alone do not establish fit or comfort.

03

How should the clip layout be approved?

Record clip count, spacing, size, coating, opening force and attachment method, then test security and edge lift on the intended wearer profile.

04

Can heat-friendly synthetic hair be styled?

Only within the tested care protocol for the actual production fibre. The source reference recommends 275–300°F, but DS HAIR must validate its own sample before publishing a maximum temperature.

05

What supports reliable reorders?

Retain an approved sample and written specification covering fibre, length, grams, base, clip map, colour, care wording, packaging and QC tolerances.

PREGUNTAS DEL COMPRADOR

Antes de solicitar una cotización

Is this 22-inch synthetic clip-in available for wholesale and private label?

It is presented as a B2B development reference. Share the target market, quantity, colour direction, fibre requirement and packaging brief so feasibility and commercial terms can be confirmed.

How many clips are on the reference product?

The source reference documents seven pressure-sensitive clips across a one-piece base. DS HAIR production clip dimensions, spacing and holding performance require sample approval.

What are the reference dimensions and weight?

The source product page states a 22-inch length, an 11 × 6.5-inch base and a 3.7 oz finished weight. Production tolerances are confirmed in writing.

Can this synthetic hairpiece be heat styled?

The source reference recommends 275–300°F. That range is not automatically a DS HAIR production claim; the selected fibre and sample must pass a documented heat test first.

Can buyers request custom colours and packaging?

Colour matching and private-label presentation can be reviewed against physical references, artwork and quantity requirements. Available options and minimums require written confirmation.
