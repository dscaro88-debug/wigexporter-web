PACKAGING QUESTIONS

Before development begins

¿Una marca de cabello pequeña o nueva puede solicitar empaque de marca privada?

Yes. We can review a right-sized label, card or packaging brief. The most practical format depends on the product, quantity range, artwork and target market.

¿Publican un MOQ o precio fijo de empaque?

No. Minimums, unit cost, sample cost and lead time depend on structure, material, printing and quantity. They are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

¿Qué archivos de logo debo proporcionar?

A vector file such as AI, EPS, SVG or print-ready PDF is preferred. If only a raster logo is available, file quality must be checked before artwork begins.

¿Se puede aprobar el empaque antes de un pedido repetido?

Packaging layout and physical presentation should be reviewed as a separate approval reference alongside the product specification.
