PUNTOS DE EVALUACIÓN

Qué debe verificar su equipo.

Use una muestra representativa para convertir las impresiones visuales en una referencia de producto aprobada y repetible.

01

Lace type & hairline

Confirm 13×4, 13×6, 5×5 or 4×4 lace, knot size, plucking depth and baby hair along the perimeter.

02

Hair quality & density

Assess fibre direction, end fullness, shedding, tangle resistance and heat-styling response on a physical sample against the stated 180%.

03

Colour & length

Match the natural black shade and the selected 16–32 in length to a physical reference before production.

04

Comfort & fit

Check cap circumference, strap adjusters, comb placement and all-day wear profile for the target market.

Does DS HAIR certify the 12A grade claim?

The 12A label comes from the reference listing. DS HAIR confirms hair grade and origin in writing against the selected supply batch rather than from a catalogue label.

Which lace constructions can be developed?

The reference covers 13×4 and 13×6 lace-front versions plus 5×5 and 4×4 closure versions. Availability, weight and price differ by construction and are confirmed per order.

Can DS HAIR develop private-label packaging?

Label, packaging, hang tags and care cards can be reviewed as part of a B2B programme. Feasibility and minimum quantities are confirmed separately.

What colour matching process do you recommend?

Use the online DS HAIR Colour Chart 2 to shortlist shades, then approve a physical colour ring or confirmed product sample under suitable lighting.
