Resumen para comprador: Lista de verificación práctica para evaluar muestras de pelucas: fibra, construcción, color, densidad y acabado.

Define the intended wearer first

Record the wearer profile, coverage need, target price position and sales channel. Without this context, feedback such as “too dense” or “not natural enough” is difficult to translate into a repeatable product decision.

Inspect the base before the hairstyle

Review base dimensions, lace or monofilament areas, parting, perimeter, clips and comfort. Photograph measurements and construction details so later discussions refer to the same points.

Review density and hair direction in real conditions

Check the product from several angles and under neutral light. Look at parting behaviour, front and crown density, movement and how the hair or fibre sits after normal handling.

Treat colour as a physical reference

Screen colours are not sufficient for repeat buying. Compare the sample against an agreed colour ring or physical shade reference, noting roots, highlights, undertone and lighting conditions.

Create an approval record

The approved sample should have a product code, dated photos, specification notes and a clear list of accepted or corrected details. This record becomes more valuable than a verbal approval when the range grows.

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Qué es lo más importante al revisar una muestra de peluca?

El paso más importante es comparar la muestra contra un brief escrito de usuaria y producto. La calidad se juzga por ajuste, construcción, acabado y repetibilidad.

¿Por qué los compradores deberían conservar una muestra aprobada?

Una muestra física aprobada y un registro escrito proporcionan una referencia compartida para futura producción, control de calidad y reorders.
