Frequently asked questions

What should a professional buyer check in a wig or topper sample?

Review the base construction, dimensions, hair direction, density, colour, attachment, comfort and finish against the intended wearer and price position.

Do you support custom wig and topper development?

We can review custom product, colour, label and packaging requirements. Feasibility is confirmed after the technical brief.

Are these products intended for retail consumers?

WigExporter is a B2B sourcing website for professional buyers, brands, distributors and salons. We do not operate as a consumer checkout store.
