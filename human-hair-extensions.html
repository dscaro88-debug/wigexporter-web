BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Which extension methods can WigExporter supply?

Our extension scope includes Clip-in, Tape-in, K-tip, Genius Weft, Machine Weft and Nano Ring. Hand-tied weft construction is available within the machine weft discussion. Exact construction, length, colour, weight and packaging are confirmed against your brief.

Can I request private-label hair extensions?

Yes. Private-label feasibility can include product specification, labels, packaging and colour direction. Minimums and lead time depend on the approved specification.

Can I review samples before a wholesale order?

Yes. We recommend reviewing the relevant construction, shade and packaging direction before repeat production.
