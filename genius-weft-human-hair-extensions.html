PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

Use the page to make a better buying decision.

Practical guidance for salons, distributors and private-label teams. Product-specific claims still require sample approval.

01

What is a Genius Weft?

Genius Weft is a continuous weft development format used in professional extension ranges. The photographed DS HAIR reference shows the general construction direction; seam profile, cut handling and installation performance must still be reviewed on the requested sample.

02

How should a buyer choose length and weight?

Length and gram weight should be selected around the intended service, natural-hair density, desired result and number of rows. A 50 g reference is useful for construction review, but it is not automatically a complete-head recommendation.

03

Why use a physical colour reference?

Hair colour changes with lighting, screens and fibre direction. Use Colour Chart 2 to shortlist shades, then approve a physical colour ring or product sample before production and repeat orders.

04

How should textures be developed?

Straight, Body Wave and curl patterns should be treated as separate approved references. Confirm pattern definition, finish, handling and care expectations on a sample instead of assuming one texture specification transfers to another.

05

What should be fixed before quotation?

Product construction, material direction, length, pack weight, colour reference, texture, packaging, estimated quantity and target market should be written into one brief. MOQ, price and lead time can then be quoted against the same document.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote

Can I order a Genius Weft sample before a wholesale programme?

Yes. Share the target market, preferred length, colour direction and evaluation goal. Sample availability, cost and timing are confirmed against the requested specification.

Can DS HAIR develop private-label packaging?

Label and packaging requirements can be reviewed as part of a B2B programme. Feasibility, minimum quantities and lead time are confirmed after the packaging brief.

Is every specification shown available for immediate order?

No. The page shows a 22-inch, 50-gram blonde reference. Final material, colour, length, pack format, MOQ, price and lead time must be confirmed in a written quotation.

Can I request Genius Weft from 16 to 28 inches or in a 100-gram pack?

You can include 16 to 28 inches and 50 or 100 grams in the buying brief. Only the 22-inch, 50-gram reference is currently documented on this page; all other combinations require written feasibility, sample and quotation confirmation.

How should I select a colour from DS HAIR Colour Chart 2?

Use the online chart to shortlist a colour code, then compare a physical colour ring or confirmed product sample under suitable lighting. Screen colour should not be treated as final approval.

Can Body Wave or Curly textures use the same specification as Straight hair?

No. Each texture should be treated as a separate development reference. Pattern definition, finish, handling and care expectations should be reviewed on the requested sample.
