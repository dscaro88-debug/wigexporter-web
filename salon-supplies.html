BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Which salon supplies can be sourced?

The range mirrors the D.S Hair Beauty salon-supply structure: five method-based kits and eight single products covering rings, tape tabs, clips, keratin, heat protection, loop tools and tint brushes.

Can you build salon starter kits?

Yes. A kit should be built around the installation method, user level and intended sales channel. Contents and packaging are confirmed before quotation.

Can supplies be ordered with hair extensions?

A combined sourcing brief can be reviewed so the accessories and consumables align with the extension methods in your range.
