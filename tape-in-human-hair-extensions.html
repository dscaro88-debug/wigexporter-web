PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

Use the page to make a better buying decision.

Practical guidance for salons, distributors and private-label teams. Product-specific claims still require sample approval.

01

What types of human hair tape-ins are available?

The range can be developed as classic sandwich panels, invisible ultra-thin panels, re-tapeable panels, sensitive-scalp panels or fashion colour/ombre panels. Each type serves a different wearer profile and salon workflow.

02

How many tape-in pieces does a client need?

Fine hair may need 40–50 pieces for a full look, medium hair 50–60 pieces and thick hair 60–80 pieces. Two pieces form one sandwich, so a 40-piece pack creates 20 sandwiches.

03

How do classic and invisible tape-ins differ?

Classic tape-ins use a standard PU base with medical-grade adhesive. Invisible tape-ins use a thinner, often translucent base to reduce the visible seam at the root. Hold, residue and re-tapeability must be tested for each.

04

What controls a reliable reorder?

Keep one approved sample and one written specification covering panel width, grams per piece, adhesive type, base colour, hair details, colour reference, tolerances, packaging and QC checkpoints.

05

Can tape-in hair be reused?

Yes, if the panels are designed as re-tapeable. Stylists remove the panels, clean residue and apply new adhesive tabs. Re-tapeable panels can extend the useful life of the hair and create recurring sales of replacement tape.

06

What should a buyer check before approving colour?

Match the base/root colour to the client’s natural hair and confirm any balayage or ombre transition against a physical reference. Screen colours are not reliable for final approval.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote

Do Tape-In Hair Extensions have a product code?

No. This product family is identified by its product name and confirmed specification: classic, invisible, re-tapeable or colour/ombre tape-in, followed by the panel format, length, grams, colour and packaging.

Can I request a sample pack?

Yes. Share the preferred tape type, piece count, length, target grams per piece, colour direction and any adhesive requirements. Sample feasibility, cost and timing are confirmed against that brief.

Does 40 pcs/pack define the full product?

No. The full specification also needs the construction, panel width, grams per piece, adhesive system, length, texture, colour reference and repeat-order tolerances.

Can DS HAIR develop invisible or re-tapeable tape-ins?

Yes. State the target base thickness, transparency level, adhesive strength and whether replacement tape tabs are required so the construction can be evaluated.

Are all lengths and weights shown ready to order?

No. They are buying-request options for preparing a brief. A length and gram value is not confirmed until it is tied to the chosen product type, panel spec, hair specification and written quotation.

Can I request private-label packaging?

Yes. Label, backing card, storage box and retail-pack requirements can be reviewed with the product brief. Feasibility and minimum quantities are confirmed separately.
