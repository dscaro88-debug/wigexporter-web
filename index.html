BUYER QUESTIONS

The questions buyers ask first.

Straight answers on what we supply, minimums, samples and timing. Commercial terms are always confirmed in writing against your specification.

Read the full Trade Account FAQ

What does DS HAIR supply?

Wholesale human hair extensions, lace wigs, hair toppers, synthetic hairpieces and salon supplies, plus OEM and private-label development for hair brands, distributors and salons. Availability, MOQ, price and lead time are confirmed against your written specification.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ is set per product, construction and whether the order is private-label. We confirm the exact minimum for your style and market during the specification review, and smaller development runs are often possible for new brands.

Do you supply samples, and are they free?

Yes, we supply samples, but they are not free. Human-hair products are high-value, so samples are provided at sample price plus shipping at the buyer’s cost. An approved sample becomes the reference that bulk production is measured against.

How long does sampling and production take?

Sampling typically takes a few working days to a couple of weeks, depending on construction. Production lead time is confirmed per order volume and stated in your written quotation. Express sample shipments to the UK and EU usually take 3 to 5 working days.
