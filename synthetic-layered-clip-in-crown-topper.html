PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

Use the page to make a better buying decision.

Practical guidance for salons, distributors and private-label teams. Product-specific claims still require sample approval.

01

What is a synthetic clip-in crown topper?

It is a partial hairpiece designed to add coverage or volume over the top and crown while leaving the wearer's own perimeter hair visible.

02

Who is a layered topper designed for?

A layered direction can blend with bob-length or longer layered hair when the coverage, density and finished length map match the intended wearer.

03

What construction evidence is required?

Use the real synthetic-topper underside photo or a physical sample approved by the buyer. Do not infer the base or attachment layout from the product name.

04

What should be tested after construction is confirmed?

Evaluate hold, comfort, coverage, fibre behaviour and movement on the intended wearer using the approved production construction.

05

What supports a reliable private-label reorder?

Retain the approved sample plus written tolerances for fibre, construction, density, length map, colour, care language and packaging.
